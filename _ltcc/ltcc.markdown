# Low Threshold Cherenkov Counter

The [Low Threshold Cherenkov Counter](https://www.jlab.org/Hall-B/clas12-web/specs/ltcc.pdf)
(LTCC) is part of the forward CLAS12 detector in Hall-B at Jefferson Lab.
It provides pion/kaon discrimination for CLAS12 measurements and supports
charged-particle identification over the forward detector acceptance.

| Question | Answer |
|:--|:--|
| What is it? | A CLAS12 Cherenkov detector made of six sectors with lightweight mirrors, light-collection cones, 5-inch PMTs, and magnetic shields. |
| Who is it for? | CLAS12 detector experts, analysts, and collaborators working with Hall-B particle identification. |
| Why does it matter? | The LTCC provides pion/kaon discrimination in the forward detector, supporting reliable event reconstruction and physics analyses. |
| Where to start? | See the [LTCC technical parameters PDF](https://www.jlab.org/Hall-B/clas12-web/specs/ltcc.pdf) and [LTCC talks](/home/showcase/ltcc). |

## Technical Context

The LTCC sectors are filled with C4F10 gas and are designed for pion/kaon
separation from 3.5 to 9 GeV/c. Each sector includes mirrors, Winston cones,
PMTs, and magnetic shields. My work is connected to the detector refurbishment,
operation, maintenance, calibration, and performance studies.
