## Bio

Maurizio Ungaro is a Staff Scientist in [Hall-B](https://www.jlab.org/physics/hall-b)
at [Jefferson Lab](https://www.jlab.org). His work
connects nuclear physics analysis, detector systems, and simulation
infrastructure. He develops and supports Geant4-based simulation workflows,
[GEMC](https://gemc.github.io/home/), [CLAS12 simulation releases](https://github.com/gemc/clas12Tags), Open Science Grid production workflows, and
technical documentation for users.

His detector work includes the CLAS12 [Low Threshold Cherenkov Counter](/home/ltcc/ltcc), with
responsibilities in operation, maintenance, calibration, and detector
performance. His research focuses on nucleon structure, meson
electro-production, resonance-region analyses, and the transition between
hadronic and partonic degrees of freedom.

Earlier and collaborative work includes experiments at [SPring-8](https://www.spring8.or.jp/en/)
in Japan and participation in the [Heavy Photon Search](https://www.jlab.org/physics/hall-b/other)
collaboration at Jefferson Lab.
